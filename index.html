US Open 2026 – Session 8

Closed · sold Aug 26, 2026 · +$141.22 (+55.4% on capital) · ~85 days held

Position

Two tickets to the Wednesday Sept 2 night session at Arthur Ashe (Men's & Women's Singles 2nd Round, Session 8). Section 310, Row D, seats 15–16 — Promenade level, sideline. Bought at face, $127.39 each, through the Amex Platinum cardmember channel on roughly June 2. Sold on Ticketmaster Verified Resale on Aug 26, seven days before the event.

P&L

Listed at $220 per ticket. Ticketmaster took a 10% seller fee ($22), leaving $198 per ticket, $396 for the pair. Against $254.78 face for the pair, that's $141.22 profit, about 55% on capital. The buyer saw roughly $256 per ticket after Ticketmaster's ~16.5% markup. Payout releases only after the event, so the $396 lands after Sept 2.

Still missing: purchase-side fees from the June order. The true cost basis is a little higher and the return a little lower than stated.

What happened

The June estimate was 2.4–3x face based on Promenade comps at $275–350. It didn't hold. Session 8 was one of the softer nights on the schedule; a week out, Section 310 comps clustered in the low $220s buyer-facing with a Row E pair at about $273.

The key correction mid-trade was that Ticketmaster's seller price isn't the buyer's price. Buyer pays seller × ~1.165; seller keeps × 0.90. The first listing was anchored on buyer-facing comps as if they were seller prices and was implicitly overpriced. Repriced to $220 seller (~$256 to buyer), just under the Row E comp, and it cleared within a day. The order-of-play repricing plan (raise if Alcaraz/Sinner/Gauff/Sabalenka got the night slot, cut morning-of if unsold) never had to run.

Timing: the Amex presale ran May 26 9am ET to May 27 11:59pm ET, and public on-sale was May 28. The purchase on June 2 was after both. Face-value Row D seats still sitting there a week after public sale was itself the tell that this session had no real spread.

Playbook for 2027

  • USTA announced 2026 on-sale dates on Apr 23. Expect late April 2027; set an alert for Apr 15.
  • Amex presale opened Tue May 26 at 9am ET in 2026. Expect similar late-May window. Buy in the first hour of day 1.
  • Limits are 8 tickets per session, 16 per date, and 2 sessions per Card Member. Not 12.
  • Size 2–4 pairs across different sessions. Multiple pairs in one section compete with each other on the way out.
  • Target weekend day sessions or Round-of-16 / QF nights. Avoid weekday 2nd-round nights; matchup risk is what killed the spread.
  • Check for the Amex 50%-off promenade offer on select sessions. If it recurs it halves cost basis, which matters more than seat choice.
  • Seats: Promenade Row C–E sideline. Check aviewfrommyseat.com for railing/glass obstruction before picking seat numbers.
  • Exit: seller price = target buyer price ÷ 1.165. List 10–14 days out. Cut the price morning-of rather than hold to expiry.
